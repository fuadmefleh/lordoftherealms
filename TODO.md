# Things To Do

## Economy

- [x] **Base prices learn from the market.** Every good's `basePrice` (data/playerEconomy.json) is a fixed anchor that town prices (`Economy.marketAskBase` × `SettlementMarket.scarcity`) are measured against. Let each anchor drift slowly toward what the good actually trades for across the world (a weighted average of town prices or trades, over seasons and years), so a good that is dear everywhere for years becomes "normally" dear. Keep it saved, deterministic and bounded.
  - Seen 2026-11-14 (seed 101, 240 days): boots' median town price was 95g against a 48g anchor; leather was 35g against 18g.
- [x] **Craftsmen won't sell below their materials.** A made good's price shouldn't fall below what its inputs cost at that market, plus a little for the work (the town trades in data/markets.json `recipes`, and the craft recipes). It fixes uneven margins: a cobbler turns 18g of leather into 48g boots, while a quilter turns 35g of cloth and wool into a 40g gambeson.
- [x] **Boot prices for troop kits.** Kitting recruits costs more now that every soldier gets boots. Check this once the two items above land, rather than hand-setting prices. Checked 2026-11-14 (seed 101, fed idle player, 720 days): a town militia kit is about 180–195g and a soldier's about 290–330g, the same as before the two changes. Boots run about 85g in towns because leather sits at its scarcity cap (2.2×) everywhere while hides are cheap: the tanneries can't keep up. That's a supply problem, not a pricing one.
