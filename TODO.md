Let each anchor drift slowly toward what the good actually trades for across the world (a weighted average of town prices or trades, over seasons and years), so a good that is dear everywhere for years becomes "normally" dear.   - Seen 2026-11-14 (seed 101, 240 days): boots' median town price was 95g against a 48g anchor; leather was 35g against 18g.

It fixes uneven margins: a cobbler turns 18g of leather into 48g boots, while a quilter turns 35g of cloth and wool into a 40g gambeson.

The campers are named roster households living in the tents, with fires, washing lines, wagons and beasts, and a tent marker on the world map. ## The poor pay to live; housing capacity and density (2026-11-26)

Done: every working class buys its basket from its wages, and the idle earn nothing (systems/townPoor.js, at each market turn after the labour turn). A shortfall is met in order by savings, day labour, the church's estate purse, the guilds' treasuries, the noble estate holders, the poor rate (the new *Poor Relief* law, or a town ordinance), the crown's dole (from the treasury, booked on the dole line), an almshouse's poor box (the player's gift), begging, petty theft and pawning. Whatever is still short goes unbought (`settlement.poor.cut`, read by the market's consumption). Hunger then lowers welfare (labour.json `welfare.weights.hunger`), raises sickness (illness.js) and kills the poorest in long dearths. The named poor get the dole at payday or go hungry, then starving: drawn gaunt, "starving" in their status, day labour / begging / leaving town / dying of "hunger and want" (townJobs.js `_hungerSeason`). Housing (world/townHousing.js, data/townHousing.json): dwellings by type and era, capacity, lodgers, rough sleepers, crowding's effects on rent, sickness, fire, welfare and growth (Economy.growthRoom). Weekly builders take timber and stone off the market. Denser types (row houses, tenements) go up where plots are scarce, and AI lot builds follow `nextDwellingType`. The player can build a tenement or row houses on their lot and let them by the room (lots.js `buildTenementOnLot`, `_roomsDay`). Tenement and terrace building families were added to data/buildingKit.json; they stand on lots with an overhang. The People panel has The poor and Housing cards.
