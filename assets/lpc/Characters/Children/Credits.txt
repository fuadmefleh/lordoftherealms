Child clothing, hair, and the sitting body and head
---------------------------------------------------
  Taken from the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator
  (https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator),
  cut to this folder's layout (walk 8 frames, idle the standing frame, sit 3 frames)
  and the hair recoloured to the LPC Revised hair palettes. The clothing's sit
  frames are derived from the child body's sit frames, coloured in each garment's
  own shades. Per-file rows from the generator's CREDITS.csv: CREDITS-ulpc-children.csv.
